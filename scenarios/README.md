# Scenarios

The hand-authored spine everything measurable is generated from — see
ticket 07 and `CONTEXT.md`. `inventory.jsonl` holds 80 Scenarios, one JSON
object per line: `id`, `category` (`ordinary` or `sensitive`), `topic`,
and `situation`, a few sentences of concrete detail naming who is
affected, what triggered the situation and what they want.

Sensitive means a property of the *question* a Scenario gives rise to —
money movement, fraud, KYC-equivalent identity verification — never a
property of an endpoint; Nivara Desk has no capability of any of those
kinds. The 30 sensitive Scenarios span billing disputes, suspicious
account activity, account recovery and ownership, payment-method changes,
and fraudulent communications, which is enough breadth to support the
~150 hand-authored sensitive eval cases ticket 09 draws from them.

`counts.md` is generated from the inventory by
`python scripts/scenario_counts.py` and is not hand-edited; a test
regenerates it and fails on drift.

Nothing here is generated by a model. The Corpus (ticket 08) and the eval
questions (ticket 09) are generated *from* these Scenarios in separate
passes and never from each other, so a question and the document
answering it share a situation rather than a vocabulary.
